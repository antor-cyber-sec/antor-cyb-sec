# Antor's Cybersecurity Learning Journey

> Learning in public: from zero to Bug Bounty Hunter and Purple Teamer.

## About me
I am learning cybersecurity from zero, studying 2 hours every day. My long-term goals are to become a professional **Bug Bounty Hunter** and a **Purple Teamer** (Red Team + Blue Team), with a focus on **AI Purple Teaming** (testing and defending AI systems).

I write every lesson in my own words and practice it hands-on. All practice is done legally and ethically: on my own computer, in labs, in CTFs, and in authorized bug bounty programs only.
